PHOTO MEMORIES FOLDER:
Place your sister's photos in this folder (e.g. photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, etc.)
or in public/photos/.

Then open:
src/data/memories.ts (or src/data/memories.js)

Update the MEMORIES_DATA array with:
- title
- caption
- date
- image path (e.g. '/src/assets/photos/photo1.jpg')
- tag (e.g. 'Childhood', 'Celebration', 'Smiles')
